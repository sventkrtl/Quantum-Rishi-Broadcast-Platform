# Business Constitution

## Metadata

- Product: Quantum Rishi Broadcast Platform
- Document Category: Executive
- Constitutional Layer: Layer 1 - Business Constitution
- Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Defines the commercial and customer context for the platform without prescribing implementation.

## Scope

Covers target industries, target customers, personas, positioning, solved problems, value proposition, commercial philosophy, supported workflows, and expansion philosophy.

## Audience

- Founder
- Product Owner
- Architect
- System Integrator
- Future Contributor

## Responsibilities

- Identifies broadcast operations as the primary business context
- Defines customers that need reliable overlay control and repeatable graphics operations
- Frames value around reliability, configurability, portability, and maintainability
- Keeps commercial direction aligned with product boundaries

## Boundaries

- Does not define licensing
- Does not define pricing
- Does not define deployment model
- Does not define implementation technology

- Commercial product clarity
- Platform boundaries before sales expansion

## Design Principles

- Single Responsibility
- Composition over Duplication
- Event First
- Configuration First
- One URL
- Platform First
- Renderer Last

## Quality Expectations

- Useful to product owners and integrators
- Stable under technology replacement
- Specific enough to prevent scope drift

## Rules

- Target customers must fit broadcast graphics and control workflows
- Commercial claims must be supported by product boundaries
- Expansion must not weaken platform principles

## Extension Rules

- New industries or workflows require product-level approval
- Expansion must preserve one URL, configuration-first behavior, and platform independence

## Stability Rules

- Business positioning remains product-level and does not depend on implementation choices
