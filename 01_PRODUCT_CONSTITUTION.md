Covers product rules, documentation philosophy, product constraints, architectural constraints, and the relationship between documentation and implementation.

- Defines documentation as the source of product truth
- Defines the hierarchy from constitution to release
- Defines what documentation must and must not contain
- Protects product boundaries from undocumented change

- Consistent authority for product decisions
- Durable product language
- Clear separation between product and implementation

## Rules

- Every architectural decision exists in documentation before implementation
- Implementation must conform to documented architecture
- Product constraints require formal documentation change before alteration

## Extension Rules

- New constitutional rules must state purpose, scope, impact, and related documents
- Major architectural changes require an Architecture Decision Record

## Stability Rules

- The constitution remains authoritative across technology replacement and product expansion
